COASTAL AIR DEFENSE COMMAND · SECTOR 7

HORMUZ

ASYMMETRIC DEFENSE

Every interceptor costs real money. The enemy is betting you go broke first.

  • Deploy. Click a concrete pad on your coast to build a CIWS (cheap drone killer), a Patriot (kills anything, $4M a shot) or a HIMARS.
  • Control the burn. Click a Patriot to switch AUTO ↔ HEAVY ONLY, so it doesn't fire $4M missiles at $20K drones and $5K decoys.
  • Hunt launchers. When an enemy launcher fires, it shows a red ping for 10 s. Click it to send a HIMARS salvo. About 3 salvos kill a launcher: $3M bounty and smaller waves.
Win: survive 10 waves or destroy all 5 launchers. Lose: all 3 coastal buildings fall, or debt goes past −$20M.
