COASTAL AIR DEFENSE COMMAND · SECTOR 7

HORMUZ

ASYMMETRIC DEFENSE

Every interceptor costs real money. The enemy is betting you go broke first.

  • Deploy. Click a concrete pad on your coast to build a CIWS (cheap drone killer), a Patriot (kills anything, $4M a shot) or a HIMARS.
  • Control the burn. Click a Patriot to switch AUTO ↔ HEAVY ONLY, so it doesn't fire $4M missiles at $20K drones and $5K decoys.
  • Hit back. A firing launcher shows a red ping for 10 s: click it for a HIMARS salvo, or select your carrier to send an F-35. Orange pings are underground missile bases, which only an F-35 bunker buster can destroy.
Win: survive 10 waves or destroy all 5 launchers. Lose: all 3 coastal buildings fall. You can borrow up to $20M; past that, weapons hold fire until income comes in. Seize the islands in the strait for extra income.
